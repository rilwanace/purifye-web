Under the Hood
Proper accounting. Hidden complexity.
You see simple cards. Underneath, it's a full double-entry system your accountant would approve of.
📒
Double-Entry Journal
Every transaction creates balanced debit and credit entries. Trial balance is always zero. Your accountant gets clean books.
📦
WAC Inventory Costing
Weighted Average Cost per product, recalculated on every purchase. COGS computed automatically per sale — no manual cost allocation.
🔄
Undo Any Transaction
Made a mistake? Hit undo — all journal entries reverse cleanly. No manual corrections, no dangling balances.
🏗️
Multi-Stage BOM
Make a chicken patty first, then use it in a burger. The system chains recipes — raw material costs flow through every stage to the final product.
⚡
Auto-Conversion at Sale
Selling a finished product with no stock? The system prompts to convert raw materials using your recipe. One tap — materials deducted, finished goods created, sale recorded.
