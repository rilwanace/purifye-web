How It Works
From idea to published. In minutes.
Upload a photo or type a brief. Social Bot handles the rest — captions, hashtags, scheduling, publishing.
01
Tell the bot
Type what's happening or upload a photo. "Fresh croissants just came out" is enough.
02
AI writes 6 captions
Social Bot generates 6 platform-ready variations. Different hooks, different angles.
03
Edit visually
Drag your caption anywhere on the photo. Pick font, color, size. Full-screen editor.
04
Pick your style
Switch photo treatments — original, vivid, warm, cool. One tap to transform the look.
05
Approve & schedule
Set hashtags, pick the time, choose platforms. Approve and it's queued.
06
Auto-publish
Connected to your API? It posts automatically. No API? Download and post manually.
Features
Become a content creating monster
AI-powered content creation, visual editor, smart scheduling, and real analytics. All from your phone.
AI ENGINE
6 Captions, One Tap
Type "new product drop" and get 6 platform-optimized captions instantly. Instagram gets hashtags and emoji. Facebook gets storytelling. TikTok gets trending hooks. All tuned to your brand voice.
EDITOR
Full-Screen Visual Editor
Your photo fills the screen. Drag caption text anywhere. Change fonts — sans, serif, mono. Pick colors from your brand palette. Toggle a pill background for readability. Pinch to zoom and reposition.
CALENDAR
AI-Planned Content Calendar
Social Bot plans your week automatically. A rolling 7-day view shows what's scheduled on each platform. One photo creates posts for IG, FB, and TK — same image, different captions.
ANALYTICS
Real Numbers, Not Guesses
Published count, reach, engagement — pulled directly from platform APIs. Stacked charts show which platform is performing. Tap any metric to drill down by day.
AUTO PUBLISH
Connect Once, Post Forever
Link your Instagram, Facebook, or TikTok account. Approved posts publish automatically at the scheduled time. No copy-paste, no switching apps, no forgetting to post.
