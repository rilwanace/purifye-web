How It Works
Six steps. Customers retained.
From importing your customer list to sending personalized messages — here's the daily flow.
01
Import Your Customers
Upload a CSV, POS export, or Excel file. Smart column mapping auto-detects names, phones, emails, and visit history.
02
Auto-Segmentation
Customers grouped by recency — Active, Warm, Cooling, Cold, Inactive. You see who's drifting before they're gone.
03
Bot Watches for Risks
Customers going cold? Birthdays coming up? Loyal customers who haven't reviewed? The dashboard tells you what to act on.
04
Pick a Message Type
We miss you, promo, exclusive days, review request, or referral. Pick the right message for the right moment.
05
Personalize & Queue
Choose a template, tweak the wording, pick recipients by segment. Messages queue up with each customer's name filled in.
06
Send via WhatsApp
One tap opens WhatsApp with the message pre-filled. Or copy and send through any channel you prefer.
