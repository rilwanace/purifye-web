You created the listing, set the price, added your photos, and waited for orders. Nothing. You search for your own product on Daraz and have to scroll through pages to find yourself — or cannot find your listing at all. This is the most common frustration for Sri Lankan Daraz sellers, and it is almost always solvable. Daraz's search algorithm has specific inputs, and optimising those inputs has a direct and measurable effect on your ranking and order rate.

01
Understand what Daraz search actually ranks on

Daraz's search ranking is not purely keyword-based like Google. It combines keyword relevance with seller performance metrics to decide who appears first. The key signals: product title keyword match, seller rating, order volume and velocity, click-through rate, conversion rate, and fulfillment metrics (dispatch speed, cancellation rate).

This means two things. First, your title and category must match what buyers search. Second, your listing performance — sales, reviews, rating — compounds over time. A listing with 50 sales and a 4.7 rating outranks a better-optimised listing with 5 sales. The best strategy is both: get the keywords right from day one and generate early sales to build momentum.

Daraz search also has a strong recency component in some categories. New listings sometimes get a brief visibility boost — use this window by ensuring your listing is optimised before you first publish it, not after. A poorly optimised listing during the initial boost period wastes the advantage.

The seller metrics that most directly affect ranking: order rate (how often people who see you buy), rating score (keep above 4.5), cancellation rate (keep below 2%), and late dispatch rate (keep below 5%). These are visible in your Seller Centre — check them monthly.

Ranking is half keywords, half performance. You control both — but they work on different timelines.

02
Write a title that matches what buyers actually search

Go to Daraz right now and search for your product as a buyer would. Note the suggestions that autofill. Note the titles of the top 5 listings. Note the words and phrases used. That is your keyword research — free, current, and specific to the Sri Lankan Daraz market.

Your title should be 100 to 120 characters and include: the primary search term, key specifications (colour, size, material where relevant), and any variant or use-case term buyers commonly add ('for women', 'xl', 'original', 'Sri Lanka'). Do not include your brand name if it is not a search term buyers use.

Bad title: 'Lotus Beauty Serum'. Good title: 'Vitamin C Brightening Serum 30ml — Reduces Dark Spots — Sri Lanka Skin | Lotus'. The second title matches the search terms buyers use ('Vitamin C serum', 'brightening', 'dark spots'), includes a key spec (30ml), and adds a local relevance signal.

Do not stuff keywords unnaturally — Daraz penalises this and it reduces your click-through rate from buyers who can see the title. The test: does a real buyer searching for this product look at this title and know they found what they want?

Your title is the bridge between what the buyer searches and your listing — build it from their language, not yours.

03
Select the correct category and fill all attributes

Listing in the wrong category is the most common reason a technically correct listing does not appear in search. Daraz search is category-scoped — when a buyer filters by category, only listings in that category appear. A kitchen tool listed under 'Home Decoration' is invisible to buyers filtering for kitchen products.

Browse the category tree carefully before listing. Look for the most specific subcategory that fits your product — Daraz rewards specificity in category selection with better relevance matching. 'Skincare > Serums > Vitamin C Serums' outperforms 'Beauty > Skincare'.

After selecting the category, fill every attribute field the listing form offers: material, colour, size, weight, brand, origin, age group, use case. Unfilled attributes mean your listing does not appear when buyers filter by those attributes. A buyer filtering for 'colour: white' will not see your white product if you left the colour attribute blank.

This step is tedious and most sellers skip it or fill it partially. The sellers who fill every attribute correctly are the ones who appear in filtered searches — where buyer intent is highest and conversion rates are best.

Category and attributes are not optional fields — they are the filters that determine who can even see your listing.

04
Generate early reviews systematically

A listing with zero reviews is at a structural disadvantage. Buyers use reviews to reduce risk, and Daraz's algorithm uses review count and score as a ranking signal. Your first 10 reviews are disproportionately important — they determine whether your listing enters a growth phase or stagnates.

The most effective way to get early reviews as a Daraz seller: reach out to buyers via the Seller Centre message system after confirmed delivery. A simple message: 'Thank you for your purchase — if you are happy with your order, a short review would help other buyers find us.' This is within Daraz's communication guidelines and has a meaningful response rate.

Do not ask for a 5-star review specifically — this can be flagged by Daraz's review moderation. Ask for honest feedback. Most satisfied buyers, when asked directly, will leave a positive review. The buyers who leave negative reviews without being asked are motivated by frustration. The ones who leave positive reviews without being asked are motivated by exceptional service.

Prioritise generating reviews on your highest-margin products first. A review-rich high-margin product significantly outperforms a review-poor low-margin one for overall profitability.

Your first 10 reviews are the most valuable marketing investment you can make on Daraz.

05
Price competitively — not cheaply

On Daraz, price is visible and comparable across all listings in a search result. Buyers see your price next to your competitors' in a single scroll. Being significantly more expensive than comparable listings hurts your click-through rate. But being cheapest is not the goal — being perceived as the best value at your price is.

Check your top 5 competitor listings regularly. Where do you sit? If you are 20% more expensive than similar products with fewer reviews, you need either better justification in your listing (better photos, more detailed description, faster delivery) or a price adjustment.

Daraz's promotional tools — flash sales, bundle discounts, platform vouchers — can temporarily boost your ranking and generate the early order volume that improves your long-term position. A brief promotional period at a lower price that generates 20 to 30 orders and 10 to 15 reviews pays back in improved organic ranking for months.

Price anchor: if you have a variant range (small, medium, large), listing the most attractive price in the search result title (which shows the lowest price in your range) increases click-through even if the specific size the buyer wants costs more. This is a legitimate tactic that Daraz's platform design supports.

Price is a signal of value, not just a number — manage it as the positioning statement it is.

06
Maintain your seller metrics above the algorithm's preference thresholds

Daraz's seller scoring system directly affects listing visibility. Sellers with strong metrics — high rating, low cancellation rate, fast dispatch, low late delivery rate — receive ranking advantages across their entire catalogue, not just individual listings. Sellers below threshold ratings see their entire catalogue depressed.

The metrics to watch monthly in your Seller Centre: Seller Rating (target above 4.5), Cancellation Rate (target below 2%), Late Dispatch Rate (target below 5%), and Return Rate (target below 5%). If any of these are trending in the wrong direction, find and fix the cause before it affects your ranking.

Dispatch speed is particularly impactful. Daraz's algorithm rewards sellers who confirm and hand over orders within the committed handling time. Set your handling time to what you can genuinely achieve — if you can consistently dispatch within 24 hours, set 24 hours. Do not set 48 hours to give yourself buffer if you typically dispatch in 12.

Every time you improve a metric that is pulling your score down, the ranking benefit appears within 2 to 4 weeks. Seller Centre metrics are a lever — sellers who watch them and act on them outperform sellers who treat them as passive scorecards.

Your Daraz seller metrics are not a report card. They are a ranking input you control.
