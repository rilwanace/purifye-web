Most Sri Lankan online sellers who aren't getting orders are either selling on the wrong platform for their product, posting content that doesn't trigger a purchase decision, or running ads before they've validated what converts organically. Here's how to fix each.

01
Master organic social before spending on ads

The majority of Sri Lankan online sellers who get consistent orders build their business on organic content first: Facebook posts (on personal profile and Page), Instagram Reels, and TikTok videos. Zero cost. Reach is determined by how shareable and engaging the content is.

Facebook has 15 million SL users. Instagram has 8.5 million. TikTok has 10.5 million. The reach is there — what's missing is content that stops the scroll.

Content that converts in Sri Lanka: Short Reels showing the product in use (30 seconds, authentic, not over-produced). Unboxing videos for fashion and electronics. Before-and-after for beauty and skincare. Customer testimonials on camera. Price reveals at the end of a video.

Post consistently: 3–5 times per week minimum. The algorithm rewards consistency more than perfection. Your 10th post will perform better than your first regardless of quality.

02
Post in Facebook selling groups — Aduwata.lk syndicates to hundreds

Facebook selling groups are the highest-conversion, zero-cost channel for most SL product categories. 'Best Buy & Sell Sri Lanka' and dozens of city-specific and category-specific groups have active buyers checking listings daily.

Aduwata.lk claims to syndicate listings across hundreds of Facebook groups simultaneously — reaching millions of buyers with one post. For consumer goods, fashion, and electronics, this reach can drive meaningful inquiry volume from day one.

Format that works in selling groups: clear product photo, price in the first 3 words of the caption, condition (new), delivery available islandwide, and a call to action (comment or DM). Don't bury the price.

Respond to every comment within 1 hour during active posting hours. Comments that go unanswered for 4+ hours convert at a fraction of the rate of immediate responses.

COPY-PASTE THIS — Facebook selling group post format
[PRODUCT NAME] — Rs.[PRICE] [2 sentences describing the product and why it's worth the price] Islandwide delivery available. COD available. DM or comment below to order. 🛒

A product post in 10 Facebook selling groups at 9am on a Saturday morning reaches more potential buyers than a Rs.5,000 Facebook ad.

03
Run Facebook Ads — but only after you know what converts organically

Facebook ads for SL sellers require a minimum of $5–10 per day for meaningful testing. Professional management costs LKR 20,000–80,000/month plus 10–20% of ad spend — worth it if your organic content has already proven the product converts.

The biggest ad mistakes SL sellers make: running a single ad creative without A/B testing, targeting too broadly (all of Sri Lanka), driving traffic to a page with no reviews or social proof, and expecting immediate ROAS without warming up the audience first.

DIY approach (under LKR 30,000/month): Target by interest (fashion, cooking, beauty, parenting) and by province. Run 2–3 different creatives simultaneously. Video ads outperform static images by 2–3x for product discovery. Keep the first 3 seconds of video absolutely compelling.

Install Facebook Pixel on your website or landing page before spending a single rupee on ads. Without the pixel, you're paying for clicks with no ability to retarget or measure true ROI.

04
Build a WhatsApp broadcast list — your best repeat customer channel

WhatsApp broadcast lists have the highest open rate of any marketing channel available to SL online sellers. Every customer who buys from you gets added to your broadcast list. When you get new stock, a sale, or a seasonal offer — one message reaches them all.

This costs nothing. WhatsApp Business (free app) handles up to 256 contacts per list, and you can run multiple lists segmented by product interest or order history.

How to build it: After every sale, message the customer: 'Would you like to stay updated when we get new items? Just say YES and I'll add you to our WhatsApp updates list — no spam, just new arrivals.' Most buyers who had a good experience will say yes.

What to broadcast: new stock arrivals (with photo), weekly deals, seasonal promotions before Avurudu / Christmas / 11.11. Open rate on WhatsApp broadcast is dramatically higher than any social media post's organic reach.

COPY-PASTE THIS — WhatsApp list invite message
Hi! Thank you for your order 😊 I get new items every 2 weeks. Would you like to be the first to know? Just reply YES and I'll add you to my WhatsApp updates list — no spam, just new arrivals and deals.

A WhatsApp broadcast list of 300 satisfied customers is worth more than 3,000 Instagram followers.

05
Work with a nano-influencer — costs less than you think

Sri Lanka's influencer market has nano-influencers (under 10,000 followers) willing to collaborate for as little as Rs.5,000–30,000, or for free product. Long-term ambassador relationships (3–6 months) produce 3.5x higher ROI than one-off posts.

Categories where influencer marketing works best in SL: fashion (especially imported sarees and casual wear), beauty and skincare, food and snacks, and phone accessories. Categories where it's harder: commodities, industrial goods, low-margin items.

Don't chase the biggest accounts. Chase the accounts whose followers look like your ideal customer. A 3,000-follower food account in your neighbourhood is more valuable than a 100,000-follower lifestyle account based in another city.

Agreement structure that works: product sample sent to influencer, honest post within 2 weeks, seller provides a discount code for their followers. No payment for first collaboration — if it converts, then discuss payment for the next.

COPY-PASTE THIS — First influencer outreach message
Hi [Name], I love your content! I sell [product] and I think it could be a great fit for your audience. I'd love to send you a sample for free — no obligations, just share your honest thoughts if you like it. Would you be open to this?

One honest recommendation from a trusted local account reaches fewer people but converts at a dramatically higher rate than a sponsored post from a celebrity.

06
Capture the WhatsApp number at every customer touchpoint

Every person who buys from you, DMs you, or comments on your post is a potential repeat customer. Most sellers don't capture their contact information. This is the most expensive mistake in online selling.

WhatsApp number = direct access to a warm buyer. Add them to your broadcast list. Send them new stock notifications. They already trust you — the second sale is dramatically easier than the first.

Where to capture: After completing a sale, ask the buyer if they want to stay updated. In your Daraz delivery package, include a note: 'Scan this WhatsApp QR to get exclusive deals.' On your Facebook page, link directly to a WhatsApp chat.

WhatsApp Business auto-reply: set up a welcome message for new chats. 'Hi! Thanks for reaching out. I sell [product category] islandwide with COD available. Share what you're looking for and I'll help.'

COPY-PASTE THIS — Delivery package insert message
Thank you for your order! 😊 Scan or message us on WhatsApp: [your number] Reply YES to get first access to new arrivals and deals.

Your WhatsApp number list is the only customer asset you own. Every other platform can change its algorithm tomorrow.
