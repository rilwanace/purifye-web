You don't need to be on every platform. You need to be in the right two places, set up properly. Start here and you'll be further ahead than 40% of teachers in your area.

01
Start with directories — less work, more predictable

Creating a profile on Iskolaya, Panthi, Mytutor, and Advice takes 2–3 hours total. Once it's up, it stays up, works 24/7, and reaches parents who are actively searching right now.

Social media requires creative energy. Directories don't. Do directories first — they're the online equivalent of a sign outside your house.

Directories don't require ongoing posting. Set them up once, update after each result season.

02
Set up a Facebook Page — not a personal profile

A Facebook Page for your tuition class is free and takes 20 minutes to set up. Name it after your subject and area: "Nugegoda O/L Mathematics — Mr. Jayawickrama."

Parents search Facebook for teachers. If you're not there, they find someone else. The page doesn't need to be polished — it needs to exist.

Add your results, class times, phone number, and a real photo. Not a logo. A real photo of you teaching.

Parents search Facebook for teachers. The one who isn't there loses to the one who is, regardless of who teaches better.

03
Build a broadcast list from your current parents

You already have the numbers. Create a broadcast list called "Class Updates" and add every current parent.

One message a week is enough. That's your existing audience staying warm while you build the new one.

This is your owned audience — it doesn't depend on any algorithm or platform changing its rules. Start using it immediately: class schedule for the new term, exam reminders, study tips.

Your existing parents are your most reliable audience. One message a week keeps them warm while you build the new one.

04
Understand which platform is for which audience

Facebook is where parents discover teachers and check credibility. WhatsApp is how they communicate day-to-day. TikTok and Instagram are where A/L students spend time — but not where they make tuition decisions.

Add other platforms only when Facebook and your directory listings are consistently generating inquiries.

For most teachers, Facebook + WhatsApp is the complete answer. You don't need to be everywhere.

Facebook
Where parents discover and vet teachers
WhatsApp
Where they communicate once they've decided

Facebook is where parents discover you. WhatsApp is how they stay. You need both. You don't need anything else to start.

05
Content creation doesn't require equipment

A photo of your whiteboard with today's lesson. A result message a parent sent you (with permission, with names removed). A study tip in Sinhala typed in 2 minutes.

You don't need a camera or an editor. You need a phone and something worth saying.

Good tuition content isn't cinematic — it's specific and honest. A whiteboard photo with "today we solved the hardest O/L algebra question from 2023" gets more engagement than a professional graphic.

You don't need a camera or an editor. You need a phone and something worth saying.

Your 4-week plan

Here's how to execute everything above, week by week.

week1
Claim your space
Register on Iskolaya and Panthi first — Highest parent traffic — full profile with subject, area, photo, contact
Take a proper profile photo — Natural light, neutral background, teaching space — not a phone selfie
Write your one-paragraph bio — "I teach A/L Chemistry in Nugegoda. X years. Students improve Y grade on average." Specific beats generic
~2.5 hours
week2
Set up the broadcast
Build your WhatsApp broadcast with all current parents — Your announcement channel for new content — set it up now
Create your Facebook page — Subject, area, phone, profile photo, cover image — 30 min done properly
Post your first results update — One real improvement — with permission, even just the numbers
~2 hours
week3
Ask for proof
Ask 3 parents for a Facebook recommendation — A personal post about their experience — most say yes when asked
List on Mytutor and ikman — Smaller traffic but searched — 20 min to complete each profile
Announce your Facebook page via broadcast — "I post updates and results there — I'd love it if you'd follow"
~1 hour
week4
Review your visibility
Search your own name and subject on Iskolaya — Do you appear? Photo showing? Contact info correct?
Set a monthly profile update reminder — 15 minutes once a month — add it to your calendar now
Note your first enquiry source — Ask how the first new enquiry found you — note the answer
~30 min
