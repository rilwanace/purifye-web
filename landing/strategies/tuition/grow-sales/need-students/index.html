If any of that sounds familiar — good, it means we've talked to enough teachers to know this is real. Here are 6 things that are actually working right now to fill tuition classes in Sri Lanka.

01
Get listed where parents are actually looking

Most teachers are on one platform, maybe two. Parents search across all of them — and if you're not there, they'll never find you.

Every listing needs the same 6 things — subjects, levels, location, class times, qualifications, and most importantly, your results.

Create a profile on at least 4 of these. It takes an afternoon and costs nothing.

02
Run a "results wall" on Facebook

Parents pick tuition teachers based on one thing: exam results. The teachers who fill up fastest are the ones who prove it publicly.

After every major exam result release, post a "results wall" — pass rates, A-grade counts, top scorers with consent. This isn't bragging. It's the #1 thing parents look for.

Post 2–3 study tips per week in Sinhala or Tamil. That's the content parents share with each other.

Join 3–5 parent community groups on Facebook. Don't sell. Answer questions. Be the teacher whose name is already familiar when someone asks "anyone know a good maths teacher near Nugegoda?"

03
Time your push to the enrollment calendar

If you're marketing at the wrong time of year, you're wasting effort. Enrollment follows a very specific rhythm.

Start marketing in November for January enrollment. Don't wait until January — by then the best teachers are already full.

Run crash courses during August vacation when students are free all day. Price as a lump sum (LKR 8,000–15,000) instead of monthly.

04
Offer a trial class — but make it a conversion tool

Pick a topic that shows off your teaching style. At the end, hand parents a one-pager with your results, schedule, and fee structure. Collect WhatsApp numbers.

Then — this is where most teachers drop the ball — follow up within 2 days.

That follow-up converts more students than the trial class itself. Two days. Not a week. Two days.

Copy-paste this

Hi Mrs. Perera, Kavinda did well in the trial class. We have 3 seats left in the Thursday batch. Want me to hold one?

That follow-up converts more students than the trial class itself. Two days. Not a week.

05
Make it stupidly easy for parents to refer you

Word of mouth is still #1 — especially outside Colombo. But most teachers leave it to chance.

You're not being pushy. You're giving a happy parent permission to recommend you — with specific details to pass along.

After every positive result, send this:

Time these asks to right after results come out. That's when parents are happiest.

Copy-paste this

Really happy with Nadeesha's improvement this term! If you know any parents looking for O/L maths tuition, I have a few spots open in the Saturday batch.

You're not being pushy. You're giving a happy parent permission to recommend you.

06
Go where the students physically are

Print an A4 flyer with one headline result ("92% O/L pass rate 2025"), your subjects, class times, and phone number.

Place at school gates 2 weeks before January term starts and before August crash course season.

Cost: LKR 200–500 for printing. That's it.

Schools with unfilled teaching positions are your highest-conversion spots. The national teacher absenteeism rate is 15.9% — parents near those schools are desperate for supplementary teaching.

200 flyers cost less than one lost month of fees — and reach students who never see a screen.
