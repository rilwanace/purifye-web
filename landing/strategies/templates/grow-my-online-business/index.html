Free sample — Unit economics calculation

Here's the core formula from the unit economics calculator in this bundle:

True margin per unit — e-commerce calculation

Selling price: LKR ______

Less: Cost of goods (supplier invoice): LKR ______

Less: Platform commission (Daraz 0–25%): LKR ______

Less: Packaging materials: LKR ______

Less: Courier charge (outbound): LKR ______

Less: COD rejection allocation (rejection rate × round-trip courier cost ÷ total orders): LKR ______

= True gross profit per unit: LKR ______

If your true gross profit is under LKR 200 per unit, increasing volume without improving margin will make the problem bigger, not smaller.
