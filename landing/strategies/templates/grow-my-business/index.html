Free sample — Month-end financial review routine

Here's one template from the bundle, complete and ready to use:

Run this every first Sunday of the month — allow 15 minutes

1. Students paying this month
Count: _____ students
Did anyone leave or join? Yes / No → if yes: _______________

2. Income received (actual, not expected)
Total collected: LKR _____________
Overdue (not yet collected): LKR _____________

3. Total costs this month
Fixed (rent, subscriptions): LKR _____________
Variable (printing, transport): LKR _____________
Total: LKR _____________

4. Profit this month
Income − Costs: LKR _____________
Margin % (Profit ÷ Income × 100): ______%
vs last month: Higher / Lower / Same

5. One thing to watch next month
_______________________________________________

Done. Put this sheet in a folder. You now have a financial record.
If this takes longer than 15 minutes, you're overcomplicating it — these 5 numbers are enough.
