Free sample — 3-step fee reminder sequence

Here's one template from the bundle, complete and ready to use:

Step 1 — Send 3 days before the due date

Hi [Parent name], this is a friendly reminder that [Child's name]'s tuition fee of LKR [amount] is due on [date]. Payment can be made via [bank transfer / cash / LANKAQR — see details below].

[Your payment details]

Step 2 — Send on the due date (if not yet paid)

Hi [Parent name], [Child's name]'s fee of LKR [amount] was due today. Please arrange payment when you get a chance — let me know if you'd like to discuss the timing.

Step 3 — Send 5 days after the due date (if still outstanding)

Hi [Parent name], [Child's name]'s fee of LKR [amount] is now [X] days overdue. I'd appreciate if we could settle this soon — please let me know if there's something specific I can help with.

Wait at least 2–3 days between each message. Replace [brackets] with actual values before sending. Send via WhatsApp to the parent's number, not the class group.
