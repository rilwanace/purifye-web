Free sample — Menu pricing formula

Here’s the core formula from the pricing calculator in this bundle:

True selling price calculation — with delivery commission

Step 1: Ingredient cost for the dish = Rs. ______

Step 2: Target food cost % = 30% (recommended for most dishes)

Step 3: Base price = Ingredient cost ÷ 0.30 = Rs. ______

Step 4: Delivery platform commission = 25% (PickMe Food standard)

Step 5: Adjusted delivery price = Base price ÷ (1 − 0.25) = Rs. ______

Step 6: Round to nearest Rs. 50 for clean pricing.

If your ingredient cost is Rs. 150, your dine-in price should be ~Rs. 500 and your delivery price ~Rs. 665.
