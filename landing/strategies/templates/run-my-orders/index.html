Message 1 — Order confirmed

Hi [Name], thank you for your order! 🙏

Your [product name] is confirmed and will be dispatched by [date].

Delivery typically takes 3–5 working days island-wide. I will send you the tracking number once it is collected by the courier.

Message 2 — Dispatched

Your order has been dispatched today via [courier name].

Tracking number: [XXXXXXXX]

Track here: [courier tracking link]

Expected arrival: [date range]. Please let me know once it arrives safely.

Message 3 — Delivery check-in

Hi [Name], just checking — did your order arrive safely?

If everything is good, I hope you enjoy it! If anything seems wrong, please message me right away and I will sort it out.
